# Pitch

Twenty words, for the GitHub About field, a link preview, or the first line of a post.

> Most visualisers just animate. This one runs 94 algorithms, measures them against their own big-O, then tests whether you understood.

## Why this one

It leads with the thing no other algorithm visualiser does. Animating a sort is
the common part — what is unusual here is that the site runs each algorithm at a
range of input sizes, counts the work it really does, draws the predicted curve
over the measurement, and says plainly when the two disagree. Then it asks you
questions about it.

That is the reason to star it, so it is the reason the pitch opens with.
